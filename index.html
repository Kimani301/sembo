What is Osembo?

The complete school management app

Osembo Comprehensive School runs on Osembo, a simple but powerful app that replaces piles of registers, fee books and mark sheets. Admin, teachers, the secretary and parents each get their own login with only what they need.

  • Record fees in seconds: Cash or M-Pesa
  • Mark registers and enter marks on your phone
  • Message parents directly by SMS
  • Print timetables, class lists and reports as PDF

FAQ

Common questions

Is it really free to install?

Yes. There is no app store and no payment to install Osembo.

Does it work without internet?

You can open the app offline from the saved copy. Some actions, like sending SMS, need a connection.

Will it install on iPhone?
