One place for the paperwork that eats your week

Teachers, the secretary and the head teacher each sign in and see only what they need. Parents get their own simple view of their child.

Marks and report cards

Enter marks per subject, rank a class or a whole list, and print report cards. Upload a whole term's results from a spreadsheet and preview them before saving.

Fees and collections

Record school fees, remedial payments and other money the school collects. See who has paid and who still owes, class by class.

Daily school life

Take attendance, share the timetable, issue textbooks to learners, and post announcements everyone can read.

What each person can do

Every account has its own sign-in and its own set of tools.

Head teacher / Admin

Full control of the school

  • Add teachers; the app creates their login password
  • Assign subjects and classes to each teacher
  • Upload students, marks and book lists from one spreadsheet
  • Set remedial lessons as morning or evening, with times
  • Give a teacher full rights, or view-only access to every class
  • Promote classes at year end, with a confirmation first
  • Read the activity log and download or restore backups

Teachers

Class teachers and subject teachers

  • Enter marks for the subjects you teach
  • Take attendance and see your own timetable
  • Record fees and remedial payments for your own class
  • Issue books and tick returned or not returned
  • Message a parent on WhatsApp or SMS from a student's page
  • View other classes and teachers without changing them

Secretary

The school office

  • Record school fees for every student
  • Collect other money, with names and amounts you set
  • Open every class list and download blank class lists
  • Post announcements to the whole school
  • Set your own password

Parents

A simple window into their child's progress

  • Sign in with the child's name and admission number
  • See the latest marks and report card
  • Check the fees balance and books the child has borrowed
  • Read school announcements

Built for classrooms with patchy internet

Works offline

Open it, enter marks and record fees with no signal. Nothing is lost.

Syncs on its own

When a device is back online, its changes reach every other teacher's phone.

Easy on phones

Protected

Install Osembo

It takes about a minute. Pick your device.

On a Windows laptop or desktop

Use Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Osembo installs like a normal program, with its own icon and window.

  1. Open this page in Chrome or EdgeUse the same link your school shared.
  2. Click Install on WindowsOr click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. Confirm the promptChoose Install. Osembo opens in its own window.
  4. Find it again anytimeLook in the Start menu or on your desktop. Pin it to the taskbar if you like.

On an Android phone or tablet

Use Chrome. The Osembo icon is added to your home screen, no Play Store needed.

  1. Open this page in ChromeUse the same link your school shared.
  2. Tap Install on AndroidIf nothing appears, tap the three dots at the top right and choose Install app.
  3. Tap Install to confirmWait a few seconds while it is added.
  4. Open Osembo from your home screenSign in once while online. After that it also works offline.

Questions

Do I need internet to use Osembo?

Only for the first sign-in and to sync. After that you can enter marks, take attendance and record fees offline. Changes upload when your device reconnects.

Will other teachers see my changes?

Yes. Once your device has synced, your updates show on the other teachers' phones and laptops.

How does a parent sign in?

With the child's names as they appear in the school records plus the admission number. The school office can confirm the exact spelling.

What if I forget my password?

Ask the head teacher to reset it. Secretaries can also set their own password after signing in.

Can I back up the school's data?

Yes. The admin can download a backup at any time, and Osembo reminds you when it is time for a fresh one.

The Install button does nothing. What now?

Make sure you are using Chrome or Edge, not a search app's built-in browser. You can also open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
